💵 Money

Currency: Barbadian dollar (BBD), pegged at 2 BBD = 1 USD. USD is widely accepted; you'll often get change in BBD. Tipping ~10–15% (check if already added).

🗣️ Good to know

  • Language: English (Bajan dialect).
  • Drives on the left.
  • Plugs: type A/B, 115V — same as the US.
  • Water is safe to drink.
  • "Wuh gine on?" = "What's going on?" / hello.

🏝️ Don't-miss

  • Swim with sea turtles on a catamaran cruise.
  • Oistins Fish Fry (Friday nights).
  • Harrison's Cave & the east-coast surf at Bathsheba.
  • Sunset at the beach — see the exact time above.
