🗓️ Trip Week Forecast

🏨 On Resort — Don't Miss

  • Hop between the resort's restaurants & swim-up bars — a different spot every night, all included.
  • Private beach + included water sports: kayaks, paddleboards, sailing, snorkel gear.
  • Red Lane Spa — book a couples massage early, the good slots go fast.
  • Nightly entertainment — live music, beach parties, movies under the stars.
  • Swim-up pool bar & hot tub for sunset drinks.

Exact activities & schedule vary by package — check the Sandals app or concierge desk for what's on each day.

🚕 Just Outside the Gate

Clearly off-resort — both very close by.

  • St. Lawrence Gap — Barbados's liveliest strip, an easy walk/short ride from the resort: casual bars, live music, beachside restaurants.
  • Oistins Fish Fry (Fri nights) — a short, easy ride away; the classic Bajan fish-fry-and-rum-punch night out.

✈️ Before You Fly

  • Check your passport & Barbados entry requirements — rules can change, confirm on the official Barbados immigration site or with your airline.
  • Do online check-in for your flight (and Sandals, if available) 24–48 hrs ahead.
  • Download the Sandals resort app — reservations, daily schedule & requests all live there.
  • Tell your bank/card issuer you're traveling so international charges don't get flagged or frozen.
  • Pack meds, chargers & valuables in your carry-on, not checked luggage.
  • Check whether airport/resort transfers are included in your booking, and confirm pickup details.
  • Consider travel insurance — this trip falls in Atlantic hurricane season (see ☀️ Weather above).
  • Grab a portable charger & an eSIM/roaming plan if you want to stay connected.

Plugs are type A/B, 115V — same as the US, no adapter needed. Language is English (Bajan dialect); "Wuh gine on?" = "what's up?"
